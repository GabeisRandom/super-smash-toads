# Super Smash Toads

A free, fan-made **platform fighter** you play right in your web browser. Forty-seven fighters, thirty-three Toads (one riding a cow, one carrying a tiny spirit in his backpack, one with a spirit cat floating behind her) and fourteen Yoshis, brawl across twelve stages built in a folded-paper 3D style. Knock your rivals off the stage to win. You can play against CPUs, with a friend on the same keyboard, or **online with up to 8 players**.

### ▶ [Play now in your browser](https://gabeisrandom.github.io/super-smash-toads/)

You can also download **Super-Smash-Toads.html** from the [latest release](https://github.com/GabeisRandom/super-smash-toads/releases/latest) and double-click it to play offline. Online play still needs an internet connection.

## How to win

Every hit raises a fighter's damage **%**, and the higher it gets, the farther they fly. Knock someone past the edge of the screen to take one of their lives (stocks). The last Toad standing wins.

## Modes & Modifiers

Up to **8 fighters** can play at once (local play: two players on one keyboard plus CPUs; online: up to 8 people). Click **⚙ Modes & Modifiers** in the lobby to set the rules (online, the host sets them and everyone else can look):

- **Stock** (lose all your lives and you're out) or **Timed** (3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes with endless lives: whoever has the most KOs when time runs out wins, then the fewest falls). After a timed match the scoreboard shows everyone's KOs, falls, self-KOs, damage dealt and taken, plus a few awards.
- **Teams:** up to four teams (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green), picked on each player's card. Teammates can't hurt each other (not even with shots, ice soldiers or finales). Works with Stock and Timed: the last team standing wins, or the team with the most KOs added up.
- **Items:** On, Off, **Smash only** (only Smash Mushrooms, now and then) or **Hyper Smash only** (Smash Mushrooms all the time).
- **Stage hazards** on or off, and the battle **music**.
- **Double length:** the stage is twice as long, with twice as many platforms (and the edges of the screen move out to match). Works on every stage.
- **OP & Early Access fighters:** fighters tagged **OP** (Dash Yoshi) or **Early Access** (Emilia Toad, still being balanced, and Water Yoshi, who still has a few placeholder moves) can be turned off for the match. The tag shows at the right of the fighter's description.

In the lobby you can also pick **Random** (a surprise fighter when the match starts). CPUs come in **levels 1 to 9** (click a CPU's level to make it harder, right-click to make it easier): 1 barely fights back, 5 is a fair match, 9 parries, punishes and guards the ledge. Above 9 is **GOD**, which reads every attack, parries almost everything, punishes every slip and is a little tougher and stronger than everyone else: good luck. Below 1 is **Dummy**, which just stands there (great for practising combos). During a match a small **Cooldowns** panel on the side of your screen shows your own moves recharging (only you see it). Press **P** to pause (online, only the host can, and it pauses everyone); change the key in Settings.

## Controls

| | Player 1 (and online) | Player 2 (same keyboard) | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|---|
| Move | `A` `D` | `J` `L` (or arrow keys) | Stick / D-pad |
| Up (also jumps) | `W` | `I` (or ↑) | Stick up |
| Jump only (never aims moves up) | `Space` | | Y |
| Crouch · double-tap to drop through a platform | `S` | `K` | Down |
| Attack | `Q` | `U` | A |
| Special | `E` | `O` | B / X |
| Guard · parry · dodge | `F` | `H` | Left bumper / triggers |
| Grab | `G` (or Guard + Attack) | `Y` | Right bumper |
| Taunts | `1` `2` `3` `4` | `7` `8` `9` `0` | Right stick |
| Pause menu · pause · mute | `Esc` · `P` · `M` | | |

These are the default keys. Change them in **Settings** on the main menu (saved in your browser).

- **Combos:** tap Attack again and again for a 3-hit combo. Hold a direction while attacking for different moves.
- **Air attacks:** jump, then press Attack: alone for a neutral air, or with a direction for forward, back, up and down airs. They're different moves from the ones on the ground (spins, flips, tucked kicks, plunges). Jump with `Space` and holding it won't turn your air attack into an up air.
- **Grabs:** grab a foe right in front of you, even one hiding behind their shield (you can also grab straight out of your own shield). Press Attack to pummel, or a direction to throw them forward, back, up or down. If you get grabbed, mash any button to break free sooner.
- **Smash attacks:** tap a direction *and* Attack at the same moment. Keep holding Attack to charge it up for a bigger hit.
- **Specials:** press Special alone, or with Left/Right, Up or Down. That's four unique moves for every fighter. Your up special is also your way back to the stage.
- **Parry:** press Guard just before a hit lands to cancel it and stun the attacker. After each try the parry needs a moment to recharge (a successful parry is ready again at once), so mashing Guard doesn't work.
- **Dodge:** hold Guard and tap a direction to roll, or press Guard in the air to air-dodge. Dodging again and again tires you out: each roll in a row is slower with less invincibility.
- **No spamming:** the same move used over and over gets weaker (the game remembers your last nine moves that landed). Mix it up and every move stays strong.
- **Knockback** starts really light at low damage and gets much stronger as the % climbs, so early hits set up combos and big hits at high % send people flying.
- **Taunts:** Booty Shake, Laugh, Yawn and Backflip.
- **Getting back to the stage:** jump again in the air, then press Up + Attack for a lift (once per trip, only when you're off the stage). Press Jump while touching the side of the stage to wall-jump, then use your up special. Ledges grab themselves, even while you're rising. Getting hit gives all your jumps back. Cowboy Toad's lasso, Toadsworth's cane and Purple Yoshi's Venom Lash can hook a ledge from a distance.

## Every fighter fights differently

- **Their own moves:** each fighter has their own set of normal attacks, built from about 85 different moves (every Yoshi has its own too: Red Yoshi breathes fire, Yellow Yoshi kicks with its boots, Purple Yoshi whips its tongue, Yoshi smashes eggs, Baby Yoshi throws a tantrum): punches, flurries, drop kicks, sword slashes and thrusts, tail whips, tongue lashes, head bonks, energy palms, drills, geysers and more. Every move has its own animation and its own swoosh.
- **Elements:** every fighter's hits carry an element you can see (and sometimes feel). Fire hits hit harder, Ice smash attacks freeze, Poison (and Stink) smashes and combo finishers poison, Electric hits shock, Wind sends foes flying flatter and farther, Rock smash attacks are heavier, Music gives longer reach, Shadow and Light hit a little harder, and Steel hits are tough and solid. The lobby shows each fighter's element.
- **Voices:** every Toad yelps "hup!", "hyah!" and "WAAAH!", and every Yoshi chirps and cheers "Yo-shi!", each in their own pitch (Toadsworth is old and wobbly, Baby Yoshi squeaks, Robo Toad and Mech Yoshi beep). They shout on big attacks, grunt when hit, cheer on taunts and wins, and say hi when you pick them.
- **Animated:** fighters breathe, blink and look around, and each has their own way of standing (Toad bounces like a boxer, Toadsworth leans on his cane, Ninja Toad crouches low, Rocker Toad nods to the beat) and running (Captain Toad and Robo Toad stomp, Ninja Toad sprints with his arms back, Yoshis scamper). They squash when they land, stretch when they jump, flip on double jumps, and flail when they fall.

## The Smash Mushroom

Every so often a glowing rainbow **Smash Mushroom** drifts onto the stage. Hit it three times to claim its power (you'll start to glow), then press **Special** to unleash your fighter's own finale.

Set **Items** to **Smash only** in Modes & Modifiers for a mode where the Smash Mushroom is the only item, or **Hyper Smash only** to have one on the stage all the time.

## Win streaks

Win match after match in a row and the results screen and lobby show your streak (🔥). Your best streak is saved in your browser.

## Items

- **Super Mushroom:** grow huge and tough. You take less damage and hit harder, but you move slower.
- **Mini Mushroom:** tiny and quick, but you take more damage and hit softer.
- **Poison Mushroom:** shrinks you and hurts. Avoid it!
- **1-Up:** an extra life.
- **Golden Mushroom:** heals some damage.
- **Super Star:** a few seconds of invincibility.
- **Turnips:** pick one up and press Attack to throw it.

## Stages

- **Toad Town:** Wiggler parades through town (hit him three times and he gets angry), and treasure chests drop goodies.
- **Captain Toad Diorama:** spike traps flash a warning, then pop up. Blocks move around, and turnip sprouts grow.
- **Toad's Turnpike:** karts race across the road, and dash panels fling you along it.
- **Mushroom Gorge:** bouncy mushrooms on both sides, and strong gusts of wind push everyone sideways.
- **Yoshi's Island:** a wide grassy island with floating islets. Now and then a giant boulder rolls across: jump over it!
- **Rainbow Road:** a glowing road through space with dash panels and rainbow platforms drifting around it. Nothing below but stars.
- **Boo Mansion:** a haunted hall with bookshelf balconies and a swinging chandelier. The lights flicker, then go out, and Boos swoop through the dark.
- **Bowser's Castle:** stone and chains over a sea of lava. When the lava rises over the floor, get up on the platforms!
- **Frostbite Glacier:** a shelf of blue ice under the northern lights. The floor is slippery (you slide when you stop), ice floes drift overhead, and now and then a row of icicles shakes loose and crashes down (their spots are marked first, and a platform above you keeps you safe).
- **Gearworks Clocktower:** inside a giant clock. Conveyor belts carry you along the floor and switch direction every so often, three platforms ride round on a turning gear, and a huge pendulum lets go from one side and sweeps across the floor: jump it!
- **Tidal Lagoon:** a sandbar at sunset with palm trees, lookout decks and rafts. The tide rolls in over the sand: in the water you float, fall slowly and move sluggishly.
- **Starlight Observatory:** a stone terrace under the stars. Gravity fades for a while now and then (everyone jumps sky-high), and falling stars streak down across the stage.

The four newest stages are original designs. Every platform can be crystallized and broken by El Minya, and Emilia's storms sweep across them all.

## Music

Pick the battle music with the **Music** button in the lobby:

- **Shuffle** (the default) plays all eight fight tracks in a random order, and switches to a new one partway through long fights.
- **Stage theme** plays each stage's own song.
- Or pick a single track: **Spore Funk** (slap-bass disco funk), **Toad Hop** (boom-bap hip-hop), **Turbo Toad** (a fast, bright chase), **Shroom Session** (acid-jazz funk), or one of the four stage themes.

The title screen, lobby and results screen each have their own music too.

**Subaru Toad** works a little like the Ice Climbers: Beatrice rides in a carrier on his back, casts his Minya and Murak spells, and follows every one of his attacks that lands with a small spark of her own. He hits softly himself, so he isn't a powerhouse, but Minya fires a fan of five quick crystals that launch harder the more damage a foe has taken, Shamac's big cloud of mist stuns for a full second, Murak carries him a long way (hold Up or Down to steer it), and the Unseen Hand reaches very far and holds its catch; keep holding Special and it slams the foe into the ground three times, then throws them a set distance. Beatrice's crystal finale, El Minya, slowly turns the platforms to lavender crystal: each crystal that lands on one spreads the crystal further (it grows crystal spikes, then trembles), the third one shatters it into shards (anyone standing there falls), and it comes back fifteen seconds later. **Emilia Toad** fights with ice, with the great spirit Puck floating behind her (every 7 seconds he holds out his paws and fires ice at the nearest foe). She has no normal attacks: her Attack button, in any direction, fires nine ice pellets that weave as they fly, home in on whichever foe is nearest, and steer around platforms (0.2 damage each, no flinch, and they last ten seconds; about 2 seconds to recharge, shown by the ring at her feet). Ice Brand Arts makes the next ice weapon in turn: a long sword that chills foes (slower for 3 s), a great axe she spins in a circle (1 s chill), a great hammer that launches upward, and a spear that skewers a foe, carries them along and bursts, freezing them for a second if they're on the ground. Ice Soldiers summons a pair of ice copies of Subaru Toad (one with two blades, one with a great axe) that fight on their own until they take three hits, four at most. Only their weapons do damage; the rest of their kit is for getting around: an ice dash, a big leap (also their way back to the stage), a low slide that slips under attacks, and a blink that melts into mist and reappears behind a foe. Each one has its own style (the blades flank, the axe keeps its distance), so they never move as one, and they walk straight through her pillar and barrier. Ice Pillar lifts her high on a pillar of ice (or, high in the air, blasts her upward), and Ice Barrier puts up a wall that curves over her head; the pillar takes 50 damage to break and the barrier 30, with a health bar on each. Her finale, Absolute Zero, locks her in place while an ice storm blows across the stage for five seconds: its pellets chip away without making anyone flinch, and its wind slowly pushes everyone the way it blows. If she uses her finale at **250% or more**, Puck becomes the **Beast of the End**: an enormous black shaggy cat with glowing yellow eyes rises behind the stage and roars, the stage goes dark and ice grows round the screen, and his storm freezes every foe a little more each moment (everything they do gets slower and slower) for twenty seconds, until they're frozen solid; two seconds later they shatter. Nothing stops it, not even Emilia getting KO'd: she comes back without Puck until it's over, and if she gets another Smash Mushroom meanwhile, it's a normal Absolute Zero. **Hasidic Toad's** up smash is a fountain of Hanukkah gelt (chocolate coins in gold and silver foil) that launches him upward; his down smash burrows underground and his down air drops a Bob-omb. The Toads of different faiths were made with the players who asked for them: their moves come from celebrations, food and music (lanterns and sweets for Eid, a wedding dance, a choir, a kung fu monk's calm, Christmas and Three Kings Day, Holi colors and the dandiya dance), never from prayers or sacred objects.

**Water Yoshi** (Early Access) fights with **Current**. Hold Special to build it up, from 0 to 100% over five seconds: his hands sway like the sea, and a faint water aura starts glowing at 20% and shines at 100%. Getting hit stops the charge but keeps what he built, and once he lets go he can't charge more until he spends it. A see-through **CURRENT** meter above his damage shows everyone how full it is (its marks at 25, 50, 75 and 100% light up as it passes them). Press Special again to release all of it, and what comes out depends on how much he had:

- **1–24%:** a small water spout that arcs down and leaves a slippery puddle for 3 seconds. Every time a foe steps in, there's a 1% chance they slip: 40% damage and six seconds lying flat.
- **25–49%:** a water blast (12%).
- **50–74%:** he stands still and fires a fast high-pressure jet that goes through everyone (18 to 25%, and it launches much harder above 140%).
- **75–99%:** a whirlpool on the floor below him for seven seconds that pulls foes toward the middle and keeps hurting them (up to 30%).
- **100%:** he winds up at his hip for a second and a half, then fires a huge steaming beam for four seconds that hits again and again (up to 50%), launches hard at the end, and **washes out** anyone still above 150%.

His down special, **Sea Dragon's Cradle**, raises a bubble of water around him (twice his size) for ten seconds. Attacks from outside can't get through, so it soaks them up (10 HP), shots that hit it heal it instead, and El Minya crystallizes and shatters it like a platform. When someone is inside, the front of the bubble fades so you can still see them. Current makes it better, and each level keeps the ones before it: extra HP, then foes who touch it are **Sunk**, then a roof you can stand on, then 30 more HP, and at 100% the water heals him 4% a second while he's inside. **Scalded** (from the big beam) burns 2% a second for ten seconds and makes the victim hit 20% softer; **Sunk** makes a foe 25% slower and take 10% more damage for five seconds. His side special (**Undertow**, a wave that rolls out and comes back, bigger if he has 25% Current to spend), up special (**Geyser Rise**, a big water jump) and finale (**Healing Tide**, which heals 70%) are placeholders for now.

**Nolan Toad** is a deadbeat after college who hasn't left his room since graduation, and he's sure he's better than you. He slouches with his hands in his hoodie pocket, a faint stink follows him around, and everyone he KOs gets stamped **LOW-TIER HUMAN**. His taunts are things he says ("low-tier human", "are you implying i'm not a high tier human?", "i'm not mad"). He's heavy and hits hard.

- **Rot Toss:** he lobs a rotten slice of pizza from under his bed (8%). Where it lands it leaves a stink cloud for five seconds that wears down anyone inside (almost 2% every half second) and slows them 20%.
- **The Shirt:** he throws the Shirt, an old T-shirt that spent a year at the bottom of his backpack. Hold Side Special and he holds it out at arm's length while it grows mold: a tap hits for 7%, a second for 14%, two seconds for 22%, and at full mold it bursts into a stink you can smell from 10 feet away (2% a second for three seconds). Keep holding past that and it hits harder (up to 28%), but the mold creeps onto him: 2% a second until he lets go.
- **Mattress Launch:** he springs high off his old mattress (12% to anyone underneath).
- **Under the Bed:** he dives under his bed (nothing can touch him for half a second) and pops out with whatever he finds: old leftovers (a stink cloud), a broken controller (he throws it, 13%) or a half-eaten bag of spicy chips (8% healed).
- **The Gap Year That Never Ends** (his finale): the stage turns into his room for eight seconds. The stink wears every foe down (20% in all) and slows them 30%, while he heals 16% (he's at home). Then the title card: GAP YEAR: DAY 1,096.

After a match he played, the results show his stats: 41,382 blitz games played, a rating of 767, and zero job applications sent.

## Playing online with friends

1. Everyone opens the game and presses **Start**. The [Play now](https://gabeisrandom.github.io/super-smash-toads/) link is the easiest way, and it keeps everyone on the same version. (The main page also has a quick "Play with friends" guide.)
2. One person is the **host**. Type your name and click **Host Online**. A 4-letter room code appears at the top of the lobby.
3. Send that code to your friends (Discord, a text, anything).
4. Each friend types their name, enters the code under **Room code**, and clicks **Join**.
5. Everyone clicks a fighter to pick it. The host chooses the stage, stocks, items and hazards, and can fill empty slots with CPUs (**Add**, then **Lv** for difficulty). Then the host presses **FIGHT!**

**Banning fighters:** the host can click **Ban fighters** at the top of the lobby, then click fighters to ban them (click again to unban, and **Done banning** to go back to picking). Anyone using a banned fighter gets switched to another one. The other players can click a banned fighter to vote to bring it back (click again to take the vote back). Once most of the people in the room want it back, counting the host, it's unbanned. With two people that means both of you; with three or four, the guests can outvote the host. CPUs don't vote.

Tips:

- Up to 8 players can be in a room (empty slots can be CPUs). The host's computer runs the match, so whoever has the steadiest internet should host.
- The host can remove someone from the room with the **Kick** button on their slot in the lobby.
- Online play connects your browsers directly to each other. If a friend can't join, have them try again. Some school or work networks block these connections, so a home network or a phone hotspot can help.
- Once you're in a room you can play match after match. After each game, press **Rematch!** or go back to the lobby to pick new fighters.
- If the game won't open for you but works for your friends, press **Ctrl+F5** on the game page to load a fresh copy, or play the downloaded **Super-Smash-Toads.html** file instead. On networks that block the online library, the game still opens and Local Play still works.

## Playing on one computer

Press **Start**, then click **Local Play**. Player 1 uses the left side of the keyboard and Player 2 the right side (see the controls above). Gamepads work too.

In the lobby, press **Change** on a slot to switch it between Player 2, CPU and empty. To choose a slot's fighter, click the slot, then click a fighter in the roster.

## About

Everything is made with plain JavaScript and WebGL: every model, stage, sound effect and song is generated in code. Open `index.html` to play from the source (it loads the files in `js/`), or run `build.ps1` to bundle it all into the single file `Super Smash Toads.html`.
