Play with friends

On one computer2 players + CPUs

  1. Click Local Play.
  2. On the second slot, press Change until it says Player 2. Empty slots can be CPUs.
  3. Click a slot, then a fighter, to choose. Then press FIGHT!
  4. Player 1: WASD, attack Q, special E. Player 2: IJKL (or arrows), U, O. Gamepads work too. Change keys in Settings.

Onlineup to 8 players

  1. Everyone types a name. One person, the host, clicks Host Online.
  2. A 4-letter room code appears. Send it to your friends (text, Discord, anything).
  3. Friends type the code under Room code and click Join.
  4. Everyone picks a fighter. The host picks the stage and presses FIGHT!
Can't connect? Try again, or use a home network or phone hotspot. Some school networks block online play.

How to play

Knock your rivals off the stage! Damage % makes them fly farther. Last Toad standing wins. Jump again in the air (Ninja and Ghost Toad get extra jumps). Ledges are grabbed automatically: press Up to climb.

Grabs: grab a foe right in front of you, even one hiding behind a shield. Then press Attack to pummel, or a direction to throw them forward, back, up or down. If you get grabbed, mash any button to break free sooner. Air attacks: jump, then press Attack (with a direction for five different ones). Space only jumps, so holding it won't turn your air attack into an up air.
No spamming: using the same move over and over makes it weaker, so mix it up! A parry needs a moment to recharge after each try, and dodging again and again tires you out. Knockback starts light and gets much stronger as damage goes up.
Attacks: tap Attack for a jab, keep tapping for a 3-hit combo. Hold a direction for tilts and aerials. Smash attacks: tap a direction and Attack together, and hold Attack to charge it up. Specials: Special alone, or with Left/Right, Up or Down, gives each fighter four unique moves. Parry: press Guard just before a hit lands to stop it cold and stun your attacker. Taunts: Booty Shake, Laugh, Yawn and Backflip. Knocked off? Jump again, use Up + Attack for a lift (off the stage), press Jump against the stage's side to wall-jump, then your up special. Getting hit gives your jumps back, and ledges grab themselves.
Items: Super Mushroom (huge and tough: take less damage, hit harder, but move slowly), Mini Mushroom (tiny and quick, but you take more damage and hit softer), Poison Mushroom, 1-Up, Golden Mushroom (heal), Super Star, Turnips. Smash Mushroom: a glowing rainbow mushroom drifts in now and then. Hit it three times to claim it, then press Special to unleash your fighter's own finale!
Stages: Toad Town (Wiggler parades through, hit him three times and he gets angry), Captain Toad Diorama (spike traps and moving blocks), Toad's Turnpike (karts and dash panels), Mushroom Gorge (bouncy mushrooms and strong gusts), Yoshi's Island (a rolling boulder: jump over it), Rainbow Road (dash panels and drifting platforms in space), Boo Mansion (the lights go out and Boos swoop by), Bowser's Castle (the lava rises: get up high!), Frostbite Glacier (a slippery ice floor and falling icicles), Gearworks Clocktower (conveyor belts, platforms riding a turning gear, and a giant pendulum), Tidal Lagoon (the tide rolls in: in the water you float), Starlight Observatory (low gravity and falling stars).
Online: Click Host Online, share the 4-letter code, friends enter it and press Join. Up to 8 players; empty slots can be CPUs (or Dummies that just stand there). Modes & Modifiers (in the lobby) sets Stock or Timed, teams, items, hazards and music. Bans: the host can click Ban fighters to ban some. Anyone else can click a banned fighter to vote to unban it, and once most of the room votes for it, it's back.
